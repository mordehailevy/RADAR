An AI multi-agent trading room
that analyzes 650+ assets every night.

RADAR collects real market data, scores every asset with a machine-learning model and eight families of analysis, then hands the best candidates to a team of AI agents that debate them and produce a risk-controlled trade plan. This page replays one real evening, the session of 30 October 2026.

Numbers on this page come from a real RADAR run. Personal data is removed and the source code is private. RADAR is a personal decision-support project, not investment advice. Its portfolio is simulated: no real money is traded.

How one evening works

From raw data to a trade plan, fully automated

A Python pipeline runs on a schedule. Every step below happens without any manual action.

Market data~650 assets: S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, CAC 40, indices, 16 commodity futures, 9 cryptos, rates, FX, volatility (Yahoo Finance, FRED, Binance).
Economy & regimeCycle phase from the yield curve, Sahm rule, unemployment, credit and inflation. Risk-on / risk-off regime.
Trader positioningCFTC COT futures positioning, put/call ratios, option walls, crypto funding and open interest.
Money flow & macro linkChaikin Money Flow, OBV, MFI, sector rotation, and each asset's sensitivity to rates, the dollar, oil and the VIX.
Machine learningGradient-boosting model retrained every night, validated walk-forward on 12 months of unseen data.
Convergence IndexEight analysis families each vote from −1 to +1. An idea is proposed only when they agree (index above 62).
AI trading roomSpecialist desks, a contrarian, a portfolio manager and a risk controller review the candidates.
Report & portfolioReports published at 21:00 and 23:45, push notifications, and a simulated €100,000 portfolio on real prices.

Risk rules

Strict limits, enforced in code

The simulated portfolio only fills an order if the real price reaches it. Stops, gaps, fees and EUR/USD conversion are included, and results are compared with the S&P 500 and CAC 40.
